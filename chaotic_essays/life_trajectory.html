A bit about me

A long thesis about how I came to be who I am

I - Who am I? Where did I come from?

My name is Jason. I'm a high school student. The thing with me is that, although I feel like it isn't obvious for me, I'm an immigrant. I was born on a summer day some time ago (but I am not about to go around telling strangers when I was born), and I came to this wonderful country called Canada on another warm summer day some other time ago, just under halfway through my life so far, more specifically to a city called Toronto, where most my memories are contained. I like this country. It's pretty flat and big; from what I've seen of my adventures through North America, Toronto is the most charming "flat and big" place I know. America is too big for me to like. Go Canada. I love this place so much.

II - How I came here

At the risk of oversharing, I'll tell you about how my parents chose to take this incredible challenge on to get us over here.

I inherited this fighting spirit from them. I fight for stuff. Without this spirit, I would not have tried to do the whole of Stardance Challenge's 30 hour certificate in a week as I am now.

My parents are pretty smart people, to say the least. So they recognized that I can get a better life here in the True North. And this is how they descended down the laundry list of countries in the West. It was actually pretty funny.

  • UK: too expensive.
  • Germany: good, but German is a small language and I wouldn't benefit from learning it.
  • USA: We actually don't really know why we didn't move to the US. I think it might have been the '16 election, actually.
  • France: the Frenchmen aren't known for their... tolerance of non-Francophones, and my parents didn't speak French.
  • Spain: I don't remember why! I think it was actually pretty high on their list. I blame Mariano Rajoy.
  • Canada: I think it was because it's an immigrant country. It's a good place, for sure.

So we chose Canada. O Canada, our home and native land...
True patriot love... in all of us command!
