Math AA HL, Physics HL: To incorrectly quote the great Ben Franklin in want of ethos, "In this world, nothing can said to be certain, except death, taxes, Math AA HL, and Physics HL." For me, it's a joke to suggest not taking these two.

All four other courses, cross-referenced against each other

Chemistry HL vs all other potential HLs: Many people take one quick look at my course and say "your grades are going to be tanked." I beg to digress, because my language grades are not great compared to my other subjects, which means that the alternative people suggest to me about "oh free grades with HL English" don't apply to me particularly well.

In any case, I enjoyed Chemistry the most last year and I feel like I did very well in it. It's therefore logical to complete my HL complement with it. Even more, STEM profile and career flexibility is great.

Global Politics vs Economics vs other Social Sciences: I didn't take Geography last year, which in hindsight is a bit of a shame in hindsight. However I don't feel like I lost greatness for mediocrity, considering I still did well in History and enjoyed it. I didn't want to continue with Geography because this means I pivot... for no particularly good reason, while having less foundations than my peers who did take it. So oh well. Taking History also wasn't something I considered strongly because I didn't enjoy it as much as I thought, and I found studying for it pretty tedious, and second only to Biology.

That leaves the choice between Economics and Global Politics. On a sidenote, I also liked them because they're new courses, and thus new beginnings. The reason why I didn't choose Economics is because I am the worst in the world at making something look nice. This is thoroughly evidenced by this website at many intervals. Unfortunately this lack of artistic skill also applies to graphs, many of which I would have to draw as part of the course, which I immediately realized would be a problem.

Of course, I didn't just choose GP because the alternative is worse. For one, I knew that this year the DP teacher for the course is rotating back to a Social Science teacher I really liked back in G8, he and I kind of are friends. Besides, I enjoy discussing and analysing politics; I don't think I have made an incorrect choice here, considering I possibly favor that over financial debates.

Additional: Why I didn't choose some courses

Biology: long names, not enough math, too much memorisation. During studying season, I had the least fun studying for this one. Besides, the prospects of a medicine-oriented path didn't appeal to me as much as the alternative.

Computer Science: I chose biology instead. Maybe for the better. It let me know how bad the other side can be and to avoid starting DP early. However, there is an argument saying I blundered, and I'll flag that as potentially reasonable.
